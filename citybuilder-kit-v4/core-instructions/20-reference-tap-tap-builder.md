## Core loop
1. **Tap to build.** The player taps buildings to construct them, and tapping speeds progress up.
2. **Tap to earn.** Buildings produce money, and the player taps to collect and grow.
3. **Hire workers** to automate the tapping (the idle layer).
4. **Offline income.** The city keeps earning while the app is closed.
5. **Handle crises** — fires, crime, unemployment — by reacting with taps.
6. **Unlock** bigger and sillier buildings, up to a spaceport, on an island metropolis.
7. **Exchange resources with friends** (light async social).

## Presentation
- Bright, blocky low-poly 3D island city with an angled top-down camera.
- Minimal HUD at the screen edges *(inferred from screenshots)*.
- Big touch targets and pop-ups for events. The map is the interface.

## Monetization
Ads (including rewarded videos for bonuses; ads can be removed by a purchase), in-app purchases and special offers.
Recent updates add **Instant Build** options and construction boosters, so speed is what's sold.

## What players praise and criticize *(from reviews and store notes)*
- 👍 Satisfying early growth, simple controls, works offline, charming style.
- 👎 The late game becomes monotonous; complaints about unreliable ad rewards and income-vs-expense balance.
- Modded APKs advertising "unlimited money" circulate, which suggests economy state that can be altered
  on the device *(inferred)*. With a player market, that would be fatal for us, so our server is authoritative.

## What we keep, change and add

| Tap Tap Builder | Our game |
|---|---|
| Tapping drives progress | **Real time drives progress**; taps are decisions |
| Money per building per second | **Recipes:** X goods per hour from Y goods, chained |
| Workers automate taps | **Workers are assigned**: more workers = faster; level = more worker slots |
| City and building upgrades by money | **No city level or XP.** Progress = research + building levels + land |
| One modern setting | **Eras:** medieval start, research moves the city through time |
| Crises fixed by tapping | Bottlenecks fixed by **planning** (stall reasons are visible) |
| Resource exchange with friends | **Open player market** with player prices (Era II) and guilds |
| Late-game monotony | A new era brings new major mechanics; old goods stay in demand |
| Blocky, cheerful island city | Keep the cheerful blocky feel, with original art, names and an era look |
