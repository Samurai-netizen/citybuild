| 1 | BalanceSim references `CityBuilder.Infrastructure` as well, only for `ContentFileLoader` | Simulation only, with its own JSON reading | The loader is strict (unknown fields, repeated keys); a copy would duplicate it and could drift. DocTools already does this. | local (`03` row) |
| 2 | Strategies also do logistics: hand trips before a Courier Station, then routes; no roads in M0-67 | Builds, upgrades, workers and research only (the task text, written before CR-0008) | Since CR-0008, goods move only with walkers: without logistics no strategy ever gets coins. `24` lists logistics. Roads stay out to keep the heuristics simple (a follow-up for M0-68 if walks dominate). | local |
| 3 | Placement: the first free tile in row-major order | A layout per strategy; distance-aware placement | Simple and deterministic; walk times differ little on 8 × 8; layouts are a later refinement. | local |
| 4 | Presence grace is a command-line option, default 300 s | Reading `appsettings.json` | It is server configuration, not content (`04` §2); the tool stays independent of the Api's settings. | local |
| 5 | Stall share is measured at the start of every step (10 s checked in, 60 s away) per producer (`StallReason.Of`), as a share of producer-seconds | Counting exact stall seconds inside the engine | No engine change; the steps are exact by split invariance (`04` invariant 2). | local |
| 6 | The run: clock 0 = the first check-in; the first check-in lasts 30 min (the first play session, golden #1), later ones 5 min; the strategy acts every 10 s while checked in; absent from check-in end + grace | One decision per check-in; first session as long as the others | Goal 1 of `24` (first coins ≈ 20 min) needs a real first session; 10 s matches the shortest cycles. All are options except the steps (`RunOptions` constants, listed in each summary). | local |
| 7 | Strategies read the content by kind and recipe (`ContentChain`): the coin chain from the currency back to raw goods; no ids in code | Naming `woodcutter`, `sawmill`, `trading_post` | `02` rule 8; M0-69 will change content, and the tool must follow without code changes. | local |
| 8 | Basic reserve: the coins of every chain type the city has none of (locked ones too) are kept back from growth, upgrades and research that doesn't unlock one; the courier station comes after every chain type | No reserve | First runs: the city spent its 800 coins on a station and a third house before Trade Charter finished and could never afford the Trading Post (purse 50). | local |
| 9 | Routes: one loop per good (producers take, waiting projects and consumers put, `all`), balanced slices beyond 8 stops, neighbouring routes joined while couriers are short; the last courier stays free for deliveries; a route only takes what it also puts | One loop over the whole chain cut into 8-stop pieces | Runs showed (a) a courier filling up with planks it couldn't drop (no Trading Post yet), (b) pieces that took logs in one route and the sawmills in another, so most sawmills starved, (c) a waiting upgrade visited before the good was picked up. | local |
| 10 | Only one project waits for goods at a time; an upgrade is only ordered when another building makes or holds its goods | Unlimited | Runs showed both builder slots blocked for days by upgrades waiting for planks (the Sawmill's own upgrade can only get planks from another building). | local |
| 11 | More couriers before more producers: when the routes the chain would need with one more building exceed the couriers (minus one), upgrade a station, else place one | Ignore courier capacity | GreedyCoins built 10 woodcutters that no route reached. | local |
| 12 | Deliveries use the cheapest amount the city may use that covers the need (presets until Tally Sticks unlocks percentages; exact once some research unlocks it), and check the courier queue first | Exact amounts | Exact is locked in M0 content (`AmountModeLocked`); the queue check removed 172 `DeliveryQueueFull` rejections. The `max(1, b × p / 100)` rule is repeated from the engine's internal `AmountRules.Of`. | local |
| 13 | `RepositoryRoot` is a copy of DocTools' ten lines | A shared tools project | Ten lines don't justify a project (`02` rule 13); noted in the module doc. | local |
| 14 | Defaults: all strategies × the three example patterns, 72 h, output `artifacts/balance-sim/` (already in `.gitignore`) | Require options | M0-68 runs exactly that; `dotnet run --project tools/BalanceSim` is then enough. | local |

## Problems and surprises
- The task text predates CR-0008: it lists no logistics, but `24` does (decision 2).
- Most of the work was strategy bugs the first runs exposed (decisions 8–12), each found by reading a run's summary and the city at the end.
- First results (72 h, for M0-68 to report properly): first research 0:00:30 and first coins 0:23:30 in every run; "once a day" earns about
  2 coins/h for every strategy (after the first session 20 coins are left, hand deliveries only happen in 5-minute check-ins, and the
  120-coin Courier Station is never affordable); with check-ins every 4 h, ResearchRush finishes all research at about 33 h. Coins pile up
  because two builder slots and 5-minute check-ins allow only a few builds per check-in, and `StorageFull:logs` is the top stall (couriers).
