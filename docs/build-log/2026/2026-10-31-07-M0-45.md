## Decisions
<!-- Every decision, when it is made, including small ones. Scope: local | project-wide (→ 12-decisions.md row) | architectural (→ ADR). -->
| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Item names are the content's, as they are: `2 Logs → 1 Planks`, not the spec's `1 Plank` | Strip a trailing "s" when the quantity is 1; add a singular name to the content | The content has one (plural) name per item; guessing English plurals breaks on other words, and a content field is a contract change that needs the owner (`15`) | local; follow-up |
| 2 | The recipe rate is `ProductionDisplay.PerHour` on a throwaway level 1 `Building` with 0 workers, asked for the level's most workers | Repeat `CeilDiv(EffectiveLabor, workers)` in the client; add a type-based helper to the engine | One source for rates (the card equals the inspector and the simulator, research included) with no engine change and no copied formula | local |
| 3 | A producer with no input reads `Nothing → 1 Logs · 120/h at 2 workers`; a house (no recipe) reads `Provides 4 workers` and has no "Max workers" line; several outputs show `rate Item` each | `→ 1 Logs`; hide the recipe line of a house | The arrow always has a left side; a house still tells the player what it is for | local |
| 4 | The hint order is the server's (`PlaceBuilding`: builder, then cost): `All builders are busy`, else `Not enough <items short>`; a locked card shows the lock and no hint | Show both hints; hint on locked cards too | One line per card; a price warning on a card the player cannot build is noise; it mirrors the error the server would answer | local |
| 5 | Lock text: `Requires: <research name>`; a type of a later era than the city's reads `Requires: <era name>`; the test is `CityQueries.IsUnlocked` (the server's rule) | Own checks in the client | The shared rule cannot drift from the server | local |
| 6 | `BuildMenuModel.TryToggle` selects a card, a second tap on it deselects; a locked or unknown card changes nothing; an unaffordable card can be selected; `Update` deselects a type that became locked | Select only; refuse unaffordable cards | `09`: hints never block, the server decides; the toggle gives the placement flow (M0-46) a cancel at once | local |
| 7 | `DurationText` shows every non-zero unit (`1h 1m 5s`), hours are the largest unit | Drop seconds above one minute | Matches the spec's `2m 30s`; no M0 content takes days | local |
