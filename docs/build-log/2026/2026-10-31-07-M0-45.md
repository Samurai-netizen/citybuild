## Sessions

### Session 1 — 2026-11-01 · Claude Code / claude-sonnet-5-5
- **Plan:**
  1. `DurationText` ("2m 30s") and `BuildCardModel` / `BuildMenuModel` (plain classes under `Presentation/Build/`): one card per building type of
     the content with name, cost, build time, max workers, a recipe summary ("2 Logs → 1 Plank · 60/h at 3 workers"; a house says how many
     workers it provides), the lock ("Requires: Trade Charter") and the hints (unaffordable, no free builder) from a city. A locked card cannot be
     selected; a hinted card still can. EditMode tests for formatting, the lock display and the hint logic.
  2. The layout: `BuildMenu.uxml`, `BuildCard.uxml` (one card, cloned per type) and `BuildMenu.uss` in `Resources/UI/`; a layout test.
  3. `BuildMenu` (thin `MonoBehaviour`): builds the cards once from the content, refreshes the lock and the hints on every `DisplayCity.Changed`,
     raises `CardSelected`; a "Build" button opens and closes it. `GameRoot` creates it once connected. Nothing is sent to the server (placement is M0-46).
  4. PlayMode test: the menu has one card per type of a sample content, a locked card ignores a tap, a refresh follows a new city.
  5. `docs/how-to/add-ui-screen.md` (this menu as the worked example) and the other pages of the Docs impact table; finish.
