| 7 | `CityCameraModel.Pan` takes a distance **on the ground**; the rig will turn a screen drag into it by projecting the old and the new pointer onto the ground plane, so the ground point under the finger stays under it | Pan by a fixed speed per pixel | Correct at every zoom and angle with no tuning number | local |
| 8 | `PointerGesture` (tap or drag, threshold in pixels) and `PinchGesture` (zoom factor) are plain classes the rig uses; a tap will select a tile, a drag pans | Put the logic in the MonoBehaviour | EditMode can test them | local |
| 9 | `TileSelection` is plain state: `Hovered`, `Selected`, `SetHovered`, `Tap`. A tap on the selected tile or outside the grid clears the selection; a coordinate outside the grid is no tile; `Changed` fires only on a real change | A separate "toggle" method; keep an outside tile | One rule for the pointer code to call; the later placement view reads `Selected` | local |
| 10 | `GroundTiles` makes 1 cube block per tile (96 % wide, 0.1 high, top at y = 0, no collider) in two greens, and two marker blocks (hover, selection; the selection one 0.02 higher; the hover one hides on the selected tile). Its materials are copies of the material a new primitive gets, so no `Shader.Find` (it could be stripped from a device build) and no new asset. It destroys what it made (`Destroy`) | `Shader.Find("Universal Render Pipeline/Lit")`; a material asset; transparent markers | Needs no shader list or asset; opaque markers avoid URP's transparent setup | local |
| 11 | Bug in my first tests, not in the code: NUnit uses one fixture object for all tests, so a stateful `readonly` field (the `TileSelection`, the `PointerGesture`) leaked state between tests (4 failed). | 12 | `CityCameraRig` (camera, selection, projection onto the ground plane) and `CityInputReader` (devices: touch first, then mouse) are plain classes; `CityWorld` is the only new MonoBehaviour (camera, sun, ground, `Update` → reader). `GameRoot` creates `CityWorld` in `Awake`, before the UI, so a camera clears the screen from the first frame (the M0-42 "Offline beside Connected" leftover) | A camera made after connecting | The camera must exist before the server answers or fails | local |
| 13 | Input: one finger or the left mouse button drags (pan) or taps (select); two fingers pinch; each wheel event is one zoom step of ×0.9 (devices report the wheel in different units, so the size is not used); the mouse pointer hovers; touch hides the hover. The tap slop is 0.06 inch (8 px at least). After a pinch the remaining finger is ignored until all are up, so it cannot tap. Bug found while writing it and fixed: a finger's release is read at its last seen position, not at (0, 0) | Scale the wheel by its value | Same behaviour on every device | local |
