The owner's Editor is not touched | Ask the owner to close the Editor; kill it | Closing or killing it is the owner's call and could lose their work; the mirror gives the same compile and test result | local |
| 6 | The 3/4 view is yaw 45°, pitch 50°, vertical FOV 40°, as named constants of `CityCameraModel` (view constants, not game numbers). The farthest zoom is the distance at which the grid's bounding circle fits the narrower screen side, with a 15 % margin (`Frame(aspect)`); the nearest is 3 world units | Orthographic camera; yaw 0 | A diamond grid is the usual city-builder look; perspective gives depth to the placeholder boxes; fitting by aspect makes a portrait phone see the whole 8×8 grid | local |
| 7 | `CityCameraModel.Pan` takes a distance **on the ground**; the rig will turn a screen drag into it by projecting the old and the new pointer onto the ground plane, so the ground point under the finger stays under it | Pan by a fixed speed per pixel | Correct at every zoom and angle with no tuning number | local |
| 8 | `PointerGesture` (tap or drag, threshold in pixels) and `PinchGesture` (zoom factor) are plain classes the rig uses; a tap will select a tile, a drag pans | Put the logic in the MonoBehaviour | EditMode can test them | local |
