## Problems and surprises
- **P1 — engine bug found by split invariance (both contents).** `Simulator.StartEligible` makes **one** pass in ascending id. A start takes
  its inputs, which can make room under a cap for a producer with a **lower** id that the pass has already skipped. That producer then stays
  idle, although it could run and shows no stall reason, until the next event of any kind. A settle that ends at that second and the next
  settle (which starts with a start pass) restart it, so splitting the time changes the result. Smallest case (golden-v1, seed 249, clock
  629053, total 130 s): woodcutter (id 1, 2 workers) idle, logs 498 of 500, sawmill (id 3) producing until 629183. At 629125 the woodcutter
  fills logs to 500 and stops. At 629183 the sawmill completes, the pass skips the woodcutter (500 + 1 > 500), then the sawmill starts and
  takes 2 logs (498). One advance of 130 s leaves the woodcutter idle until the sawmill's next completion at 629363. Advancing 130 s then
  0 s starts it at 629183. Over 595 s the split city ends with 500 logs and the whole one with 498. The fixture fails the same way (wood 96
  against 98). Checked: repeating the pass until nothing starts makes every Simulation test green (local change, reverted).
