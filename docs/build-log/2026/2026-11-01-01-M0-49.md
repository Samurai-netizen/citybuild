## Decisions

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The plan board row of M0-48 goes from `In review` to `Done` in this PR (the protocol: the next task does it once the owner merged) | Leave it | `16` §E.7. The build-log index lines of M0-47 and M0-48 still say `In progress` although both entries are Done: corrected here too | local |
| 2 | Chips: one per item of the city's era or an earlier one, in the content's order (coins, logs, planks in the fixtures). The stock and the cap are plain numbers, the rate is `ProductionDisplay.NetPerHour` | Only items with stock or a rate; a hand-written list | The spec says "one chip per item". Coins have a chip too. A hand-written list would put item ids in code (principle 8) | local |
| 3 | `PerHourText` shows whole items, or one tenth when the rate is not whole (`+12.5/h`), rounded toward zero; a rate under a tenth reads `0/h` and counts as flat; the minus is U+2212 as in the spec's `−30/h` | Whole items only; two decimals | A producer slower than one item an hour (the engine keeps thousandths for that) would read `0/h` in whole items. The text and the trend come from the same rounded value, so a `0/h` is never coloured red or green | local |
| 4 | The connection indicator is a dot and a word driven by `ConnectionState` (`Online`, `Connecting`, `Offline`, `Signed out`, `Update needed`, `Server problem`), a second input to `HudModel.Update` beside the displayed city | Show the connection from `DisplayCity`; reuse `ConnectionStatusModel`'s long sentence | The spec lists the indicator and the display city cannot know the connection. The long sentence (with the error code) stays in `ConnectionStatusView` | local |
| 5 | The model type for the rate text is `PerHourText`, the HUD component will be `HudView` | `RateText` / `Hud` | A static class `RateText` clashed with the property `ItemChipModel.RateText`; a class `Hud` in the namespace `Hud` would shadow it | local |
