     playerId, phaseEndsAt) → 400 with time elapsed and nothing stored; negative and huge coordinates → `OUT_OF_BOUNDS`, nothing stored;
     a replay storm (sequential and concurrent) → one building, one cost, one receipt, identical bytes; a huge display name and a 1 MB body
