## Problems and surprises
- `new-log-entry.sh` exits 1 silently when no entry exists yet for today's UTC date: under `set -euo pipefail`,
  `ls docs/build-log/<year>/<date>-*.md` fails on no match, so the `ls | wc -l` pipeline fails. Not OS-specific.
  Proposed fix: `n="$( (ls … 2>/dev/null || true) | wc -l | tr -d ' ')"`.
- GitHub: `main` has no protection; the two rulesets (`test-1`, `test-2`) target `refs/heads/test`, not `main`.
  The repository is public (`18` says private). Merge commits and rebase merges are allowed; head branches are not auto-deleted.
- `guard-bash.sh` false positive: a single Bash call that contains `git push` anywhere and a force flag anywhere
  else (here: inside a heredoc of log text) is denied as a force-push. Workaround: keep pushes in their own call.
- `M0-PLAN.md` at the repo root is a byte-identical duplicate of `docs/plan/M0-PLAN.md` (two homes for one fact, `17` §2).
