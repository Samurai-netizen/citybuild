## Decisions
<!-- Every decision, when it is made, including small ones. Scope: local | project-wide (→ 12-decisions.md row) | architectural (→ ADR). -->
| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't commit the FBX files of the Blender re-run | Commit them so the files match the last build | Same geometry (same sizes ± the path, `strings` diff shows only `CreationTime` fields and the texture `FileName`); committing would put one machine's absolute path (`/Users/...`) into 57 binaries for nothing | local |
| 2 | The road preview is the level 1 road model with its arms, in the ghost materials of `PlacementGhosts` (depth-only + see-through, `PlaceholderArt.CreateGhostMaterials`) at alpha 0.25, pooled in `GroundTiles` | A new shader that draws on top (`ZTest Always`); one see-through material only | The art's URP Lit has a fixed `ZTest LEqual`, so lifting is the only way over the grass without a new shader; one see-through material alone blends the road's layers over each other (the reason `PlacementGhosts` has two); pooling keeps a drag free of new objects | local |
| 3 | Lift the road preview and the path markers by `OverlayLift` = 0.09 (the tallest grass detail of visual v2 is 0.084, measured from the FBX files); the hover and selection markers keep their height | Lift every marker; draw the preview at the road's own height | The owner named the road preview; the trip path and the existing-road picks use the same yellow markers and were buried the same way; lifting the hover and selection squares would hide each tile's grass under an opaque square, a look the owner did not ask for | local |
| 4 | Existing roads picked for an upgrade or a removal keep the (lifted) yellow markers; only new roads show the see-through road | A see-through road of the next level over them | A pick for a removal shown as a better road would be wrong; a see-through road over the same road shows nothing | local |
| 5 | `InspectorFlowTests.BuildRoads_FromBuildMenu_…` now expects `ShownRoadPreview` with 3 tiles and `ShownPath` empty instead of `ShownPath` with 3 | — | The rule changed on the owner's request (issue 1), not to get green; the test keeps every other assertion | local |
