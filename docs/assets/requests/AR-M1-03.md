## Notes for the asset model
- Everything here lies on the ground under the city; it must stay quieter than the buildings (lower, flatter, less contrast) so buildings stay readable.
- Tiles sit edge to edge with `tile_grass` and its variants: same top height, same bevel, the same rim colour where they meet grass.
- The map is `core-instructions/29-era1-chronicle.md` → Map: forest and rock in clumps, a river running north to south, fertile soil on the east bank.
